What It's Designed To Do

One operating environment, not a stack of dashboards.

Prakut One is still being built. Here's the direction it's headed in.

  • Farm Management SystemsA single system of record for what's happening on the farm, replacing scattered notes and phone updates.
  • Farm DataCost, activity, and yield data captured as it happens, not reconstructed after the fact.
  • GISSpatial mapping of land, plots, and infrastructure for planning and tracking.
  • IoTField sensors feeding real conditions back into the system where it's useful, not for its own sake.
  • AutomationReducing manual, repetitive reporting work for on-ground teams.
  • AI-Assisted Decision SupportSurfacing what needs attention, rather than just logging what happened.
Our Philosophy

We don't believe technology fixes farms. Better decisions do.

Prakut One exists to support the judgement of the people running your farm — not to replace it. Technology is useful only when it changes what actually happens on the ground.
