About Prakut

A farm is not just land. It's an operating business.

Large farms in India have the resources to perform — good land, available water, willing labour — but lack the systems to make it happen. Prakut bridges that gap with structure, clarity, and accountability.

Most people see

Land → Crops → Harvest

Prakut sees

Land → Assets → Operations → Production → Markets → Revenue → Profitability

Our Story

Built by someone who has lived it.

Most farm management companies are built by people who studied agriculture. Prakut was built by someone who has lived it — on his own land, with his own money. Not advising on it. Not studying it. Running it.

We go beyond advisory. We assess a farm, design systems tailored to its land and market access, and execute them on the ground with a trained team. We don't step away after planning — we stay, optimise, and refine until the numbers move.

Our Expertise

Five disciplines, one team.

  • AgricultureCrop planning, soil management, and on-ground farm operations.
  • TechnologySystems and data infrastructure for real-time farm visibility.
  • ProcurementEfficient sourcing and supply chain cost optimisation.
